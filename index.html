Questions you might have

  • Q: What is this site?
    A: This is a fan project to provide transparent pricing, correct images, and user friendly information about the Atomic Shop in Fallout 76.
  • Q: how does it work?
    A: The site uses in-game data which gets compiled with the help of a member of the Fallout 76 community.
    Images and missing images are pulled from the game files and then reordered manually to match the order of the included items in the shop.
    Prices are converted to various currencies based on SteamDB Atom pricing. Daily offers compiled manually and stored as data to be automatically updated.
  • Q: How do I use this site?
    A: You can browse the Atomic Shop by clicking on the tabs at the top. Click on an item to see more details, including prices and images.
    You can also use hotkeys to scroll through the tab bar and navigate images using A and D to move left and right.
  • Q: Are prices official?
    A: Atom Prices are pulled from in-game data and should be accurate to the time of the last update.
    Conversion to other currencies is based on SteamDB Atom pricing and may vary by region and might not be correct.
  • Q: How often is the site updated?
    A: The site is usually updated on Tuesdays, but may take several hours depending on the extent of changes that have to be made.
  • Q: Why is there an/no Weekly Offers Previer tab?
    A: The Weekly Offers tab provides a quick overview of the current week's daily offers for the upcoming days.
    The data is compiled from fallout.bethesda.net/news/ if available and then shown or not shown depending on the availability of data.
  • Q: Can i share single items or bundles?
    A: Yes, you can share items or bundles by clicking the share icon in the item overlay upper left corner.
    This will open a text box with a direct link to the item that you can copy and share.
  • Q: Who is behind this project?
    A: This site is created and maintained by a member of the Fallout 76 community, with help from another member who provided the core data
    and GitHub Copilot for scripting assistance.
  • Q: How can I support this project?
    A: You can support this project by sharing it with others, providing feedback, or contributing in any way you can.
    I may consider adding a donation option via Ko-fi or similar platforms to help cover hosting and domain registration costs in the future.
    No contact informations yet.
  • Q: Is there an GitHub repository for this project?
    A: Yey, the project is on GitHub but not open to the public yet.
    Once it's ready for public access, I will provide a link here and allow contributions from the community
    for better item images and other improvements.
  • Q: Can I buy items directly from this site?
    A: No, this site does not support direct purchases. You must visit the official in-game Atomic Shop in Fallout 76 to buy items.
  • Q: Is this site affiliated with Bethesda?
    A: No, this site is unofficial and not affiliated with Bethesda.
  • Q: How can I report issues or suggest improvements?
    A: You can report issues or suggest improvements via the Feedback Form or by contacting the site owner directly.
