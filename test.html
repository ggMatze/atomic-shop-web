Saber Color

Sets the blade light color on the Jedi Challenges lightsaber over Bluetooth. You don't need the old app or the headset.

This browser can't use Bluetooth from a web page. Use Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS or ChromeOS. On iPhone, use the free Bluefy browser. The page must be opened over https:// or localhost.
